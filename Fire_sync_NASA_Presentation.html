Our Innovation

Fire sync: Autonomous Planetary Intelligence

Harmonizing multi-sensor NASA Earth observations into life-saving ground decisions in under 5 seconds.

🛡️ Tactical Command Hub (Responders)
  • Multi-Sensor GIS: Unifies MODIS (1km) & VIIRS (375m) thermal radiometric footprints.
  • Satellite Overpass Ticker: Predicts the exact minutes until the next orbital reconnaissance window.
  • Atmospheric Vector Radar: Live MERRA-2 and GOES-16 wind speeds, gusts, and KBDI drought indices.
  • Ground Truth Verification: Drone and ranger photo feeds that eliminate false thermal positives.
👤 Civilian Safety Portal (Communities)
  • Panic-Free Interface: Strips away technical jargon to present actionable, immediate safety status.
  • Local Air Quality Alerts: Sentinel-5P aerosol indexing and PM2.5 respiratory warnings.
  • Safe Evacuation Corridors: Dynamic routing steering citizens away from flame fronts to emergency shelters.
  • Offline-First Resilience: Embedded dataset caching ensuring the platform never fails during network outages.

Space-Based Telemetry

NASA Data Assets & Partner Missions

Multi-mission fusion providing continuous spatial, temporal, and spectral coverage.

🇺🇸 NASA Primary Space Assets
  • NASA FIRMS (VIIRS 375m I-Band): Flown on Suomi-NPP & NOAA-20/21 for high-resolution fire perimeters.
  • NASA FIRMS (MODIS 1km Thermal): Flown on Terra & Aqua for broad thermal anomalies & radiative power (FRP).
  • NASA POWER & MERRA-2: Climatological surface wind velocities, relative humidity, and solar radiance telemetry.
🇪🇺 Space Agency Partners & In-Situ Data
  • ESA Sentinel-2 (MSI 10m): Multi-spectral NDVI vegetative monitoring & post-fire burn scar rehabilitation.
  • ESA Sentinel-5P (TROPOMI): Atmospheric NO2 and aerosol smoke index for downwind health advisories.
  • Open-Meteo & OpenStreetMap: Real-time localized weather telemetry and civil infrastructure layers.
